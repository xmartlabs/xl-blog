## How we use Claude internally

Claude Code is part of how our engineers work across the development lifecycle. We also keep our own repo of skills and AI practices, and when the tools we needed didn't exist, we built them and open-sourced them.

[Cursor Plugin CC](https://github.com/xmartlabs/cursor-plugin-cc) connects Claude Code and Cursor. Review gives an independent second opinion on your code, with a severity level for each finding. Delegate runs tasks in the background while you keep working.

[Gnomon](https://github.com/xmartlabs/gnomon) reads the AI-agent transcripts already on an engineer's machine and turns them into a profile that engineer can inspect. It runs locally and is open source under the MIT license. Federico Balarini, our Head of Engineering, puts the goal this way: "A score is a prompt for reflection, not a verdict."

## What it means for our healthcare clients

Healthtech has been our main focus for the past few years. It's also where AI gets hard fast. Protected health information can't go wherever a prompt sends it. Outputs have to fit EHR workflows. A clinician won't adopt a tool that adds clicks to their day.

That's the context for most of our client work:

* [**Altro Pharmaceuticals.**](https://xmartlabs.com/work/altro-pharmaceuticals) From a legacy platform to a pharmaceutical marketplace.
* [**Care coordination.**](https://xmartlabs.com/work/ai-care-coordination) A platform that turns field conversations into actionable care.
* [**Redi.**](https://xmartlabs.com/work/redi) AI-powered preparation that helps patients get ready for medical appointments.
* **Elemind.** The companion app for a wearable that helps people fall asleep faster. The full case study is coming soon, so stay tuned!

Our patient intake work is a good example of the constraints at play. It collects clinical information through voice and text and produces structured, EHR-ready outputs without sending PHI to the cloud.

![Voice and text input turned into a structured checklist that stays local instead of going to the cloud.](/images/claude-partner-network-patient-intake.png)

If you're deciding whether Claude belongs in your healthcare product, you work with a team certified by Anthropic that has already shipped AI features under PHI and EHR constraints. We start with a short discovery phase to decide whether Claude fits your use case and which workflow to tackle first.

## What it means for our AI product clients

Not every client is in healthcare. Many come to us to build AI-native products from scratch or to add AI to a product that already has users.

For them, the value is the same habits we use internally. When our engineers join your team, they bring project context files, plan review, and verification steps into your codebase, so the agent's output is something your team can trust and maintain. If you want to test an idea before committing to a roadmap, [AI Studio Week](https://ai-studio.xmartlabs.com/) takes a concept to a working product in five days.
